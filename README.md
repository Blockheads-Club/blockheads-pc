The Blockheads was made for phones. **Blockheads PC** brings it to your desktop: a big window or fullscreen, mouse and
keyboard, your game controller, your worlds. It runs **your own copy** of the Android game, so nothing is included
and nothing is downloaded.

> [!NOTE]
> **Alpha (v0.5.1 pre-release).** The game starts, worlds load, sound plays and online servers work. Expect a few rough
> edges, and please [tell us what you find](https://github.com/Blockheads-Club/blockheads-pc/issues).

## ❓ Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Is the game included? Do I need to buy it?</b></summary>
<br>
The game is not included. You need your own copy of The Blockheads for Android (the APK file). Blockheads PC only
runs that copy on your computer.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Windows or macOS says the app is not safe.</b></summary>
<br>

- **Windows:** the installer is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS:** the app is not notarized yet. Right-click the app, choose **Open**, then **Open** again (first time only).
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where are my worlds?</b></summary>
<br>
Press <b>Open data folder</b> on the Home screen. Controls, saves and known issues:
<a href="docs/playing.md">docs/playing.md</a>.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>The game stutters, freezes or closes.</b></summary>
<br>
Press <b>View logs</b> on the Home screen and attach them to a
<a href="https://github.com/Blockheads-Club/blockheads-pc/issues/new">bug report</a>, with your Blockheads PC
version and your system. More tips: <a href="https://blockheads.club/docs/pc-port#troubleshooting">troubleshooting</a>.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is this official?</b></summary>
<br>
No. It is a free fan project, not affiliated with Majic Jungle or Noodlecake.
</details>

## 🛠️ For developers

<details>
<summary><b>Build from source</b></summary>
<br>

You need Rust stable (1.86+), CMake 3.16+, a C++20 compiler, Boost headers, SDL2, and [bun](https://bun.sh) for the
launcher.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Blockheads-Club/blockheads-pc.git
cd blockheads-pc
cargo build --release -p bhpc-run --features play
./target/release/bhpc-run --apk "/path/to/The Blockheads.apk"
```

Before a pull request run `scripts/check-fast.sh` (every change keeps 100% test coverage). More:

- [docs/development.md](docs/development.md): how it works, build, run options, tests and coverage
- [docs/launcher.md](docs/launcher.md): the launcher app (`app/`)
- [docs/feasibility/PLAN.md](docs/feasibility/PLAN.md): architecture and milestones
</details>

Everyone can open [issues](https://github.com/Blockheads-Club/blockheads-pc/issues) and pull requests. Irrelevant or
abusive issues may be closed or removed.
